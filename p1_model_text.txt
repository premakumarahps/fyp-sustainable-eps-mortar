stiffening effect, a behavior entirely missed by simple One-Factor-At-A-Time testing.

theoretical limits simultaneously, the degradation of the Interfacial Transition Zone 
(ITZ) compounds non-linearly. The matrix transitions from a predictable structural 
composite into a highly brittle, mechanically unstable state.
