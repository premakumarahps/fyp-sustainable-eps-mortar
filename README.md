**Department of Materials Science and Engineering | University of Moratuwa**

---

### Folder Organization
You can place your project documents, research data, and reports in the respective directories:

- 📁 **`docs/`**: Final dissertation / thesis / project report PDFs, research papers, journal publications, and technical reports.
- 📁 **`data/`**: Experimental data, characterization datasets (XRD raw data, SEM/EDS, FTIR, DSC/TGA, mechanical testing, tensile/hardness logs, etc.).
- 📁 **`figures/`**: High-resolution micrographs, diagrams, schematics, graphs, and sample photographs.
- 📁 **`web/`**: *(Will be scaffolded automatically once your documents are placed)* Modern, responsive web application showcasing your research methodology, characterization results, material mechanisms, and academic findings.
