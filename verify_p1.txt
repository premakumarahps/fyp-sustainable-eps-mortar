of the moisture-compensation algorithms executed during batching, the theoretical 
fresh density (𝜌𝑡ℎ) of each Phase 1 matrix was benchmarked against its measured fresh 
density (𝜌𝑓𝑟𝑒𝑠ℎ) and 28-day hardened density (𝜌28𝑑). Establishing this volumetric 
integrity is a critical prerequisite; severe deviations between theoretical and actual 
yield indicate either inaccurate specific gravity assumptions, fluctuating fine aggregate 
moisture, or highly inefficient mechanical compaction.
